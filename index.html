About me

I am Wentao Zhang, a high-performance computing scientist with a computational physics and chemistry background. I design and implement the parallel algorithms that let scientific simulation run at scale. My core work is parallelizing relativistic density functional theory; MPI, distributed-memory algorithms with strong scaling across 1,000+ CPU cores and multiple nodes. Distributing the memory removed the per-node ceiling that had made heavy-element systems uncomputable, so calculations that were previously impossible now run as a matter of course.

Day to day that means MPI and distributed-memory design, CPU/GPU hybrid execution, profiling and eliminating bottlenecks, and numerical linear algebra at scale, written as production Fortran, C/C++, and CUDA on HPC clusters. Python is the layer I orchestrate it from: build and job workflows, regression testing, performance analysis, and training machine-learned interatomic potentials. I am actively seeking industry HPC roles — parallel computing, performance engineering, and scientific computing infrastructure — and I am equally open to research positions, research institutes, and industrial R&D groups. If you are working on problems where scaling and numerical performance actually matter, I would like to talk.
